Who we are

Turning scrap into value, responsibly.

In that regard, we collect scrap metal, paper, and plastic and process them directly or indirectly.

In 2013, we decided to start the company with one vision — a waste-free future — and the ambition to be a leader in our industry through innovation and integrity.

“Our main goal is zero waste.”

How we work

From scrap to resource.

We collect scrap metal, paper, and plastic and process them directly or indirectly — keeping valuable material in circulation.

Collect

We collect ferrous and non-ferrous scrap metal, paper and plastic from our partners across the region.

Process

Material is processed directly or indirectly, ensuring it is prepared to the standard industry expects.

Return

Premium recycled products go back into the supply chain — one step closer to zero waste.
