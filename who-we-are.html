Our story

Started in 2013 with a simple belief.

In 2013, we decided to start the company. Our vision is a waste-free future, and we want to be a leader in our industry through innovation and integrity.

We collect scrap metal, paper, and plastic and process them directly or indirectly. Today we operate from our headquarters in Panama City with offices in Costa Rica, Jamaica and Suriname.
